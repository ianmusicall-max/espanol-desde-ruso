Privacy policy

Updated: 2026-10-31.

What the site itself collects

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no forms, no comments, no accounts. We do not ask for or store your name, address or email.

Advertising

The site shows Google AdSense advertising. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

Personalised advertising can be switched off in Google's ad settings, and third-party vendors at aboutads.info. google.com/settings/ads · aboutads.info · policies.google.com

Your rights

If you are in the European Union you have the right to access, correct and erase your data. Since the site stores no data about you, such requests go directly to Google.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

The microphone

Lessons have a button to record yourself. It asks for microphone access only when you press it. The recording stays in the browser's memory, is never sent to a server and is not saved on the device: it disappears when you close the page.
